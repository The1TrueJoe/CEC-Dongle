Reference

CEC addressing guide

Logical addresses

  • 0 — TV
  • 1 — Recording Device 1
  • 4 — Playback Device 1
  • 5 — Audio System
  • 8 — Playback Device 2
  • E — Free Use
  • F — Broadcast

Physical addresses

  • 0x1000 — HDMI 1 on the TV
  • 0x2000 — HDMI 2 on the TV
  • 0x3000 — HDMI 3 on the TV
  • 0x4000 — HDMI 4 on the TV
  • 0x2100 — HDMI 1 on a device connected to TV HDMI 2
